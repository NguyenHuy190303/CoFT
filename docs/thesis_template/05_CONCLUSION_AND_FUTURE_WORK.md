# THESIS WRITING GUIDE: CHAPTER 5 - CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK

This final chapter wraps up your entire body of work. It is not merely a summary, but also an opportunity to re-assert the value of your contributions, objectively acknowledge limitations, and propose future directions for the research community.

---

### 5.1. Summary and Conclusion

**Purpose:**
To provide a concise summary of the entire research process, from the initial problem to the final findings.

**Required Content:**
- **Recap of Problem and Objectives:** Begin by briefly restating the research problem and the objectives set out in Chapter 1.
- **Summary of Methodology:** Describe your approach and experimental procedure at a high level.
- **Synthesis of Key Results:** Highlight the most important results presented in Chapter 4 and how they answered your research questions.
- **Draw the Final Conclusion:** Based on the results, provide an overall conclusion for the entire work. E.g., "In conclusion, this study has shown that applying model X to problem Y is feasible; however, its performance is significantly affected by factor Z."

---

### 5.2. Contributions

**Purpose:**
To clarify the specific, novel contributions your work brings to the research field.

**Required Content:**
List the main contributions clearly, often in bullet points for easy tracking. Contributions can be of several types:
- **Empirical Contribution:**
    - E.g., "Provided a comprehensive performance analysis of algorithm X on a new, previously unstudied dataset."
    - "Is the first study to compare the performance of models A and B under condition C."
- **Methodological/Architectural Contribution:**
    - E.g., "Proposed a new data preprocessing method that improved model accuracy by 10%."
    - "Developed a flexible system architecture that allows for..."
- **Resource Contribution:**
    - E.g., "Built and published a labeled dataset for the community."
    - "Developed an open-source toolkit for..."

---

### 5.3. Limitations

**Purpose:**
To frankly acknowledge the weaknesses or incomplete aspects of your research. This demonstrates maturity in scientific thinking and increases the credibility of your work.

**Required Content:**
- **Methodological Limitations:**
    - E.g., "The hyperparameter selection was done manually rather than using automated techniques, which may not have found the most optimal configuration."
- **Data Limitations:**
    - E.g., "The study used only a single dataset, therefore the generalizability of the results is limited."
- **Scope Limitations:**
    - E.g., "The thesis did not consider the impact of factor X, which could be an important factor in practice."
- **Briefly explain why these limitations exist** (e.g., due to time constraints, computational resources).

---

### 5.4. Future Work

**Purpose:**
To propose specific ideas and directions for other researchers (or for yourself in the future) to build upon and extend your work.

**Required Content:**
- **Stemming from Limitations:** Every limitation you noted in the previous section is an excellent source of ideas for future work.
    - E.g., If the limitation was using only one dataset -> "Future work could include testing the model on various datasets from other domains."
- **Improvement Ideas:**
    - E.g., "The model could be improved by integrating additional types of information, such as text data from reviews, using natural language processing techniques."
    - "Exploring alternative model architectures like [Architecture Name] might yield higher performance."
- **New Application Directions:**
    - E.g., "The developed framework could be adapted to address problem Y in a completely different field."
- **Be Specific:** Instead of saying "improve the model," say "improve the model by adding mechanism X."
