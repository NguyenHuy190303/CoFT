# THESIS WRITING GUIDE: CHAPTER 1 - INTRODUCTION

This chapter lays the foundation for the entire thesis. Its goal is to guide the reader from a general context to your specific research problem and to convince them that your work is important and necessary.

---

### 1.1. Background and Motivation

**Purpose:**
To set the context, explain why this research area is important, and what motivated you to undertake this topic.

**Required Content:**
- **Introduction to the Field:** Start with an overview of the relevant technology field (e.g., "In the last decade, recommender systems have become an indispensable part of e-commerce platforms...").
- **Importance:** Explain why this field is valuable in practice (e.g., "They help enhance user experience, boost sales, and personalize content...").
- **Common Problem:** State one or more common, well-known challenges in the field (e.g., "However, efficiently handling large-scale datasets with high sparsity remains a significant challenge...").
- **Motivation:** Lead into the research gap you have identified. This serves as a natural bridge to the next section.

**Tips:**
- Write this section engagingly, like telling a story, to capture the reader's interest.
- Avoid getting too technical right away. Keep it at a conceptual level.

---

### 1.2. Objectives and Research Questions

**Purpose:**
To state precisely and clearly the goal of the thesis and the specific questions it will answer.

**Required Content:**
- **Main Objective:** Present the overall goal of the research in one or two sentences. It should start with an action verb (e.g., "The main objective of this thesis is to **analyze** the performance of model X on datasets Y...", "This study aims to **build** a framework Z...").
- **Research Questions (RQs):**
    - Break down the main objective into 2-4 specific, answerable questions.
    - Each question should focus on one aspect of the problem (e.g., performance, influencing factors, scalability).
    - These questions will guide the subsequent chapters, especially the Results chapter.

**General Example:**
- RQ1: Does the [Model Name] model achieve acceptable performance on [Data Type] datasets with characteristics [A, B]?
- RQ2: Which factors in the data (e.g., [factor 1], [factor 2]) have the greatest impact on the model's performance?
- RQ3: How does the model's scalability perform as the data volume increases?

---

### 1.3. Scope and Delimitations

**Purpose:**
To clearly define the boundaries of your research. This helps manage reader expectations and shows that you have a realistic plan.

**Required Content:**
- **Scope:** Describe what the thesis **will** cover.
    - Specific technologies/algorithms being studied.
    - Datasets being used.
    - Evaluation metrics being applied.
- **Delimitations:** Describe what the thesis **will not** cover and why.
    - E.g., "This thesis will not attempt to develop a completely new algorithm, but will focus on applying and evaluating an existing model."
    - "Due to computational resource limitations, we will not conduct experiments on datasets larger than X."
    - "User interface analysis is outside the scope of this study."

---

### 1.4. Methodology Overview

**Purpose:**
To provide a brief overview of the steps you took to complete the research.

**Required Content:**
- Briefly describe the end-to-end workflow.
- E.g., "To answer the research questions, we first selected and preprocessed real-world datasets. Then, the existing model was adapted and trained. Finally, the model's performance was evaluated using metrics X and Y, and the results were analyzed to identify influencing factors."

---

### 1.5. Thesis Structure

**Purpose:**
To provide a roadmap for the reader, briefly describing the content of each subsequent chapter.

**Required Content:**
- A short paragraph for each chapter.
- E.g.,
    - **Chapter 2** presents the theoretical foundation of...
    - **Chapter 3** details the implementation methodology, including the technology, data, and experimental procedures...
    - **Chapter 4** reports and analyzes the experimental results...
    - **Chapter 5** summarizes the findings, discusses limitations, and suggests directions for future research.
